# The "Worth My Time?" Date Decision Framework

A systematic, quantitative framework designed to help you decide whether to go on a date with someone you feel ambivalent about. It removes emotional guesswork by calculating a weighted decision matrix based on your boundaries, energy levels, and opportunity costs.

## The Core Dimensions (Variables)

Rate each of the following variables on a scale from **1 to 5**.

1. **Energy Balance ($E$)**
   * *Question:* Will hanging out with this person leave me recharged, or drained?
   * *Scale:* 1 (Will drain me completely) → 5 (Relaxing / Energizing)
2. **Time Opportunity Cost ($T$)**
   * *Question:* What am I giving up to go on this date? 
   * *Scale:* 1 (Giving up much-needed rest or deep work) → 5 (I literally have zero other plans)
3. **Experience / Venue Value ($X$)**
   * *Question:* Is the date activity or location something I want to do regardless of the person?
   * *Scale:* 1 (Hate the venue/activity) → 5 (Bucket-list activity or favorite restaurant)
4. **Spark Potential / Openness ($P$)**
   * *Question:* How curious am I about them? Is there potential for a surprise connection?
   * *Scale:* 1 (Zero attraction/curiosity) → 5 (Highly curious to see what happens)

---

## The Formula

Not all variables are equally important. Your energy and the potential for a spark matter more than the venue or your schedule. We apply the following weights ($W$):

* **$W_E$ (Energy):** 35%
* **$W_P$ (Spark Potential):** 30%
* **$W_X$ (Experience):** 20%
* **$W_T$ (Time):** 15%

### Equation

$$ Score (\%) = \frac{(E \times W_E) + (T \times W_T) + (X \times W_X) + (P \times W_P)}{5} $$

*Note: We divide by 5 because the maximum value for any variable is 5, normalizing the score to a 100% scale.*

---

## The Decision Thresholds

Once you calculate your percentage, consult the thresholds:

* 🟢 **Score ≥ 70% (Green Light)**
  * **Action:** Go! 
  * **Why:** The combination of low cost, high experience value, and genuine potential for a pleasant surprise makes it a statistically good bet.
* 🟡 **Score 45% – 69% (Conditional)**
  * **Action:** Go **only** if it’s low-friction. 
  * **Why:** Apply the 90-Minute Rule. Grab a casual coffee or a single drink near your place. Do not commit to a full dinner or an exhausting event.
* 🔴 **Score < 45% (Hard Pass)**
  * **Action:** Cancel or decline politely.
  * **Why:** Protect your evening. Staying home, resting, or hanging out with friends yields a mathematically better return on your energy.

---

## Pre-Date Ground Rules (If Score is 45-69%)

If you decide to go on a "Conditional" date, protect your energy by setting these rules:

1. **The 90-Minute Cap:** Set a hard exit time *before* the date starts. ("I'd love to grab a drink, but I have an early start tomorrow so I have to head out by 8 PM.")
2. **Pre-Budget the Financials:** Suggest a casual spot where you are comfortable paying your own way. This prevents any feelings of "debt" or obligation.
3. **Zero Performative Enthusiasm:** Match their energy. Don't exhaust yourself trying to fill awkward silences or people-please. Your goal is simply to observe how you feel around them.
